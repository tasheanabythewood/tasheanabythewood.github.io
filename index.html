A city service fails every summer, and the fix is not a longer promise

517,287 Cincinnati 311 service requests. Bulky-waste collection misses its 14-day commitment in a concentrated seasonal window, and 84 percent of one year's misses fell in four months. Demand does not explain it, and lengthening the commitment had already been tried without effect.

Operations analytics · Recommendation: fund seasonal capacity, run the constraint diagnostic first

When going for it is clearly better, NFL teams still punt about half the time

47,892 fourth-down decisions, 2014 to 2025, tested against public win-probability models. Teams punt 52.5 percent of the time when going for it is better by 2+ points, and go for it 0.3 percent of the time in the reverse case. The habit has halved since 2014 but holds in every season.

Decision analytics · Recommendation: make going for it the default on fourth and short in a team's own territory
